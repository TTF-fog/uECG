## Related schematic checks

- **Battery-mode headroom:** `VOUT` is the charger's SYS output. On battery it follows the cell through the BAT FET, so U2 cannot maintain 3.3 V down to charger cutoff. Sweep cell voltage with peak load and check the transition when USB is removed.
- **USB PHY decoupling:** U4-G2 (`DECUSB`) is unconnected on the board. Confirm whether the exact HJ-840 module revision includes the capacitor internally. [Nordic specifies 4.7 µF nominal for the bare nRF52840](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/nwp_030/page/wp/nwp_030/nrf52840_decusb.html); that does not prove an external capacitor is required for the module.
- **Electrode current margin:** At 3.3 V and minimum resistance, the 330 kΩ ±1% input resistors R19/R20 give about 10.10 µA in a simple full-rail fault calculation. Recheck all three electrode paths against the AD8232 guidance using worst-case voltage and resistance. This is not a patient-safety assessment.

After DRC passes, test charging under load, USB in both orientations, and ECG noise while USB, radio, and charging are active. Use an electrode simulator and an isolated bench setup. Do not connect a person to the board while it is attached to a non-isolated USB host or bench supply.
